Questions, answered

Good to know.

Quick answers to the things people often ask.

How do I use TikDownload?

Copy a public TikTok video link, paste it into the input above, and select Check link. Download availability depends on the supported retrieval method.

Is TikDownload free to use?

The website prototype is free to use. Any future limits or costs will be clearly disclosed.

Can I download any TikTok video?

No. Only content that is accessible through a supported method and that you have the right or permission to save should be downloaded. Private videos and restricted content are not supported.

Does this work on my phone?

Yes, the site is designed to work on mobile browsers as well as desktop browsers.

Do you store the links I paste?

This prototype does not send links to a server. A download service is not connected yet.
